Start and Sparkle team, behind the scenes.

We excel in transforming both residential and commercial spaces into spotless, inviting environments.

Family-owned and locally operated in Alberta, our professional cleaning services cater to a wide range of needs, from regular maintenance and deep cleans to post-renovation and construction cleaning, as well as move-out services.

Whether it's a cozy home or a bustling office, our skilled team is committed to delivering exceptional results with meticulous attention to detail. We pride ourselves on using top-quality, eco-friendly products to ensure a safe and healthy environment for you, your loved ones, and your colleagues.

Our flexible scheduling and personalized approach mean we tailor our services to fit your specific needs and preferences, no matter the size or type of space.

Our customers are at the core of everything we do.

We are proud to serve a diverse clientele, ranging from homeowners and renters to business owners and property managers. Each client receives our undivided attention and personalized service, tailored to meet their specific cleaning needs.

We are grateful for the trust our customers place in us and strive to build lasting relationships based on reliability, quality, and exceptional customer service. Your feedback is invaluable, guiding us to continually improve and deliver the best cleaning experience possible.

Your satisfaction is our top priority.

Our team is the heart of our success.

Each member is carefully selected for their dedication, professionalism, and passion for delivering exceptional cleaning services. Our staff is highly trained, ensuring that your home or business is in trustworthy and capable hands.

We take pride in our friendly and approachable team, who go above and beyond to meet your unique cleaning needs. Our collaborative spirit and strong work ethic drive us to consistently exceed expectations.

We believe that a clean space leads to a happier, healthier life, and our team is dedicated to bringing that joy and comfort to every client we serve.
